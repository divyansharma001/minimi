# Here is our CRM shortlist for a 20-person sales team: HubSpot Sales Hub,…

**You**, 11 Nov 2026, 08:54 UTC

Here is our CRM shortlist for a 20-person sales team: HubSpot Sales Hub, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM, Freshsales and Salesforce Starter. What does each cost per user per month?

**minimi**, 11 Nov 2026, 08:56 UTC

Short answer: entry prices run from about $9 to $25 per user per month. The catch for a 20-person team is Salesforce Starter Suite, which caps at 10 users, so you would be pushed to Pro Suite at $100.

| CRM | Entry paid plan (per user/month) | Next tier up (per user/month) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HubSpot Sales Hub | Starter ~$15–$20 [13]; listed at $20, promo $7 with annual commitment [23] | Professional $100, $90 with annual commitment [23] |
| Pipedrive | Lite $14 billed annually, $24 monthly [18] | Growth $39 billed annually, $49 monthly [18] |
| Zoho CRM | $14 annual / $20 monthly; free for up to 3 users [14][20] | Professional $23 annual / $35 monthly [14] |
| Freshsales | Growth $9 annual-effective / $11 monthly list [24][11] | Pro $39 annual-effective / $47 monthly list [24][11] |
| Salesforce Starter Suite | $25 [3] | Pro Suite $100, billed annually [3] |

Things that matter for 20 seats:

- **Salesforce Starter Suite is limited to 10 users**, so 20 people means Pro Suite at $100 per user per month [4].
- **HubSpot Professional adds a one-time onboarding fee of $1,500** [25].
- **Freshsales figures conflict.** One comparison table lists it from $15 per user per month [32], while the plan pages give $11 monthly and $9 annual-effective [11][24]. Check the vendor page before budgeting.
- Salesforce's $25 entry sits above Zoho CRM's $14 and Pipedrive's $14.90 [33].

All figures are USD list prices, and the pages were checked or updated at different dates in 2025–2026, so treat them as indicative rather than final quotes.
