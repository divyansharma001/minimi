Which Node.js versions are LTS right now, and when did or does Node.js 20 reach end of life?

Three Node.js lines are LTS right now: **26 (Active LTS), 24 (Maintenance LTS) and 22 (Maintenance LTS)**. **Node.js 20 reached end of life on 30 April 2026** [5][12].

| Version | Status (5 Nov 2026) | End of life |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Node.js 26 | Active LTS since 28 Oct 2026 | 30 April 2029 [12] |
| Node.js 24 | Maintenance LTS since 20 Oct 2026 | 30 April 2028 [12] |
| Node.js 22 | Maintenance LTS | 30 April 2027 [12] |

- Node.js 26 entered Long Term Support as version 26.13.0 on 28 October 2026, codename "Lithium", and gets updates through the end of April 2029 [11].
- Node.js 20 stopped getting official patches from 1 May 2026: a vulnerability found in it no longer receives an official patch [5].
- One source still shows the older picture — Node.js 24 as the Active LTS line and Node.js 26 as the Current line scheduled to enter LTS in October 2026 — but it predates the promotion, and the newer sources above show the change [2].

If you are on 20, 22 or 24, the practical point is that only 26 has the long runway (to April 2029) [12][14].
