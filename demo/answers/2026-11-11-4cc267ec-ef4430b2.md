# Answer to: Here is our CRM shortlist for a 20-person sales team: HubSpot Sales Hub, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM, Freshsales and Salesforce Starter. What does each cost per user per month?

Per user, per month — list prices (most are annual billing; monthly billing costs more):

| CRM | Entry | Mid | Top |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Salesforce Starter Suite | $25 (capped at 10 users) [4] | Pro Suite $100, annual [3] | — |
| HubSpot Sales Hub | Starter $20 [17] | Professional $100 ($90 with annual commitment) [17][22] | Enterprise $150 [17][22] |
| Pipedrive | Lite $14 annual / $24 monthly [38] | Growth $39 / $49 [38] | Premium $59 / $79, Ultimate $79 / $99 [38] |
| Zoho CRM | Standard $14 / $20 [24] | Professional $23 / $35 [24] | Enterprise $40 / $50, Ultimate $52 / $65 [24] |
| Freshsales | Growth $11 [11] | Pro $47 [11] | Enterprise $71 [11] |

Notes for a 20-person team:

- **Salesforce won't work on Starter.** Starter Suite is limited to 10 users, so 20 people means Pro Suite at $100 per user per month on annual billing [4][3].
- **Pipedrive and Zoho** both show big annual-vs-monthly gaps. Zoho's monthly billing runs about 43–52% more on the lower tiers [30].
- **Freshsales** prices above look like monthly-billing list prices, with discounts for annual; one source lists annual as $108 / $468 / $708 per user [11][31].
- **Zoho conflict:** one source lists Enterprise at $52 [25], but two others put Enterprise at $40 annual / $50 monthly and $52 as Ultimate [24][26]. The two-source version is more reliable; confirm on Zoho's own page before you buy.
- **HubSpot** Professional is $100 per seat monthly, $90 with an annual commitment [17][22]. Prices also differ by region — new EMEA customers now buy one whole-account edition rather than separate Hubs [22].
- Ranges for Pipedrive ($14–$79 annual, $24–$99 monthly) are confirmed by a second source [34], with Premium's $59 flagged as worth confirming [40].
