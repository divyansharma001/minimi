# Chat screen (src/app/page.tsx)

Mode: Operate. Primary users: hiring reviewers testing memory by date and freshness on a laptop; later the builder's own tool.
Task: ask, watch it work, read a checked answer, open sources, reopen a past chat by date.
Pinned by the user: modern like Sarvam (sarvam.ai), grainy green gradient, white rounded cards, clean friendly sans, grey labels with bold values, tinted status pills; colour theory and colour psychology. Sarvam's brand system (github.com/ashree2118/sarvam) informs craft only: its fonts are trial-licensed and its marks are Sarvam's, so neither ships.
Build path: code-led (no image generation in this session). Direction pinned by the user, so no concept roll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm workbench where every fact shows its receipt. Refuses the category default of a dark chat column with a glowing accent; colour lives behind the work as a grainy green field, and the work sits on white paper cards.

OWN-WORLD: Sarvam's two registers. Campaign register: a fixed, grainy, analogous green field (green-100 to green-500 with a low ochre warmth) behind everything. Product register: white and #FCFCFC cards, 16 to 32px radii, barely visible shadows, text #1F1F1F never black, weights 400 to 600 only, pill buttons, tinted status pills (green checked, amber changed, soft red was wrong, grey could not confirm) with text dark enough for 4.5:1. One sans, Figtree, open licence.

STORY: The reviewer sees dated chats on the left and knows it remembers. They ask; live steps say what it is doing (finding the 3 Nov chat, re-reading sources, searching, checking). The answer arrives as one card: prose with clickable [n] markers, then each checked fact as a row with its pill, then numbered sources with the day read.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: floating white sidebar (wordmark, New chat, chats grouped Today / Yesterday / dated) at left on the green field; centre column max 760px; empty state is one large balanced line on the field plus four question chips from the brief; composer is a white pill-cornered panel docked at the bottom centre. Mobile: field, top bar with menu and wordmark, sidebar as a drawer.

FORM: Pinned by the user (no roll); ordered list position 1 of 1; seed key: none (user-pinned direction).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
